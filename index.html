Privacy Policy

Magic Piano Tiles (Magic Piano AL) · Android app com.idc.pianogameal
Effective date: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Magic Piano Tiles game ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. We built the App to work without accounts, sign-ups or any personal details from you.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store or receive any personal information on our own servers. The App has no login, no registration and no in-app forms.

The App stores the following only on your device:

  • Your best scores, stars and crowns for each song
  • Your favourite songs
  • Your settings (tile speed, volume, accompaniment and vibration)

If you have Android backup turned on, Android may back up this game progress to your Google account and restore it on a new device. Google handles that backup under its own privacy policy. We cannot access it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads, AdMob may automatically collect and process information such as:

  • Your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • Your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version)
  • How you interact with ads (views, taps)
  • Diagnostic and performance data

Google uses this information to deliver ads, which may be personalised, as well as to limit how often you see an ad, measure performance and prevent fraud. Google is the one processing this data, not us. For details, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • AdMob and data privacy

3. Your consent and choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: when the law requires it, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before ads use your data. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy & ad choices in the App.
  • US states with privacy laws: where it applies, you can opt out of the use of your data for personalised advertising through the same privacy options.
  • Everyone: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device).

4. Permissions the App uses

The App does not ask for access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or precise location.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal information. The only third party that receives data from the App is Google AdMob, as described in section 2.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your game progress stays on your device until you delete it. To erase all of it, uninstall the App or clear its data in Settings → Apps → Magic Piano AL → Storage → Clear data. Google AdMob keeps advertising data according to Google's own retention policies.

7. Security

The App only connects to the internet over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Game progress is kept in the App's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read.

8. Children's privacy

The App is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and change the effective date above. Continuing to use the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
